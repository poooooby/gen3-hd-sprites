# ⚙️ Options

[← Back to the README](../README.md)

Pick **WOOBLEVIEW** on the game's own OPTION screen, or open **OPTIONS → MODS → WoobleView**.
Pressing B there takes you straight back. Battle Textbox, Battle Scene and the five options after
it only show in FireRed, LeafGreen, Ruby, Sapphire and Emerald.

## Sharp Pokémon

| Option | Choices | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Battle HD** | On / Off | On | Battle Pokémon at full resolution. |
| **Field HD** | On / Off | On | Overworld Pokémon at full resolution. Turn it off on a slow device. |
| **Pokedex & Summary HD** | On / Off | On | Pokédex and summary Pokémon at full resolution. |

## Fitting the screen

| Option | Choices | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Battle Fit** | On / Off | On | Pokémon at your sprite mod's own size, with one too big for its space shrunk just enough to fit. The camera pulls back if anything still doesn't fit. Off: your sprite mod's own sizes. Needs Battle HD. |
| **Fill Screen** | On / Off | On | Battles and full screens fill your screen in any shape. Needs Battle Fit. Not in Gold, Silver and Crystal. |
| **Battle Textbox** | On / Off | On | Ruby, Sapphire, Emerald, FireRed and LeafGreen: the battle's text box and menus redrawn as one sharp bar across the bottom, a quarter shorter, in each game's colours; the same bar after a catch, an evolution and an egg hatching. Off: the game's own boxes. Needs Battle HD. |

More in [Fitting battles to your screen](fitting-the-screen.md).

## 3D battles

| Option | Choices | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Battle Scene** | Classic / Overworld | Classic | Overworld: battles set in the 3D world around you. Needs Battle Fit. |
| **3D Sizing** | Off / Minimal / Medium / Accurate | Medium | With Battle Scene on Overworld, how true to life the Pokémon's sizes are. Medium: real sizes, with the biggest gently scaled down to about 5 m tall and 6 m wide and the smallest raised so they're easy to see. Minimal: like Medium, with everyone drawn toward the middle. Accurate: every Pokémon at its real measured height, scaled down only to fit the screen or the room. Off: drawn and framed as in Classic battles, at your sprite pack's own sizes. |
| **Build 3D Cache** | Press A | – | Builds the 3D world now (a minute or two) instead of a little at a time while you play. |
| **Clear 3D Cache** | Press A | – | Deletes the saved 3D world. It's rebuilt when needed. |
| **Scene Blur** | On / Off | On | A soft miniature-style blur on the far and near ground. |
| **Time of Day** | On / Off | On | Outdoor battles lit by the time of day (morning, day, evening, night), by the game's clock or WoobleDex's. Off: always day. |
| **Scene Cache** | On / Off | On | Keeps built towns and rooms so battles load straight away. |

More in [3D battles](battle-scene.md).
