# 🎮 What works in which game

[← Back to the README](../README.md)

WoobleView is made for the Gen 3 games, and brings what it can to the older ones.

| | FireRed, LeafGreen | Ruby, Sapphire, Emerald | Red, Blue, Yellow | Gold, Silver, Crystal |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Sharp battle and Pokédex Pokémon | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Sharp overworld Pokémon (from supported mods) | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Fill Screen | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | – |
| Modern battle text box | ✅ | ✅ | – | – |
| 3D battles | ✅ | ✅ | – | – |

- **Gold, Silver and Crystal** keep the game's own framed battle screen, so their battles look
  the way other mods (such as Facets' battle scenes) draw them.
- **Time of day** in 3D battles follows the game's clock in Ruby, Sapphire and Emerald, and your
  device's clock in FireRed and LeafGreen, unless WoobleDex's clock is in charge.
